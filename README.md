**Dear data project**

**Front Door Colours: A neighbourhood pallete**

About

I will be creative a processing sketch that turns the front doors of my neighbourhood into a colour pallete. I will walk around and record the colour of each front door I pass. One door is one d**ata point.

Data

Each door is one row in the CSV file, with these columns: door_id, date, time, colourn and kerp appeal.

Colour: judged by eye and written as colour name such as red or blue.
Weather: written as a word, such as sunny or rain.
Kerb appeal: how nice the whole front of the house looks from the pavement, scored 1 to 3. 1 is bare or untidy, 2 is fine, 3 is really inviting.
